BL AGRO INTEGRATED LTD is a premier export and logistics firm dedicated to supplying high-grade Nigerian resources to the international market. Our primary operations focus on the bulk supply of essential Agro Commodities including Ginger, Soybeans, Hibiscus Flower, Bitter Kola, Premium Cashew Nuts, Sesame seeds, and Cocoa. In addition to our agricultural reach, we are established players in the Solid Minerals sector, facilitating the trade and export of Tantalite, Coal, Lithium, Gemstones, and Industrial Charcoal. By leveraging a robust local supply chain and strict quality benchmarks, we provide global partners with consistent, high-purity shipments tailored to industrial demand.
